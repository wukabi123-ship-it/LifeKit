What Is JPG to PDF Conversion?

JPG to PDF conversion combines JPG or JPEG image files into a PDF document. Each image can become a page in the resulting PDF, making a group of images easier to print, share, store or submit as one document.

How to Convert JPG to PDF

  1. Upload one or more JPG or JPEG images.
  2. Arrange the images in the order you want.
  3. Choose the PDF page settings.
  4. Choose how each image should fit on the page.
  5. Create the PDF.
  6. Download the finished PDF document.

Choose the Right Page Size

A4 is a common choice for documents intended for printing, school work and general sharing. Letter is another common paper format, while Original Image Size can be useful when you want the PDF page to follow the dimensions of the image.

Portrait, Landscape or Auto?

Portrait pages are taller than they are wide, while landscape pages are wider than they are tall. Auto can be useful when working with images that have different shapes.

Fit vs Fill

Fit keeps the complete image visible inside the page and may leave some empty space. Fill uses more of the page but can crop parts of the image.

Why Reorder Images?

The order of your images determines the page order in the final PDF. Reordering them before creating the document can help keep photos, scans or document pages organized.

When Is JPG to PDF Useful?

JPG to PDF can be useful for combining scanned pages, receipts, photos, notes, forms or other image files into a single document that is easier to share or upload.

Does Converting Images to PDF Change the Original Images?

The original image files remain separate from the PDF created by the tool. The PDF is generated as a new document for download.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert multiple JPG images into one PDF?

Yes. Select multiple JPG or JPEG images and they can be combined into one PDF document.

Can I change the order of the images?

Yes. Drag the image cards in the preview area to change their order before creating the PDF.

What page size should I choose?

A4 is a practical choice for many documents, especially when the PDF may be printed or shared.

What is Original Image Size?

This option creates PDF pages based on the dimensions of the source images instead of using a standard paper size.

Will the image be cropped?

Fit is designed to show the entire image. Fill uses more of the page and may crop parts of the image.

Can I add more images after uploading?

Yes. Use Add More to select additional JPG or JPEG images.

Are my original images replaced?

No. The PDF is created as a separate output file and does not replace your original image files.
