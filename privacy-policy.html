Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to LifeKit. LifeKit provides simple online tools designed to help users complete everyday tasks quickly and easily.

We respect your privacy and are committed to explaining clearly how information may be handled when you use our website.

2. Information We Collect

LifeKit does not require users to create an account to use most of its tools.

Some tools may process files or information directly in your web browser. When this happens, the information may be processed locally on your device rather than being uploaded to our servers.

We may receive limited technical information automatically through standard web technologies, such as browser type, device information, approximate location, and pages visited.

3. How We Use Information

Information that may be collected automatically can be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Improve website performance and usability.
  • Understand how visitors use LifeKit.
  • Detect technical problems and security issues.
  • Support advertising and analytics services, where applicable.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

LifeKit or third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies.

Cookies may be used to remember preferences, understand website usage, measure advertising performance, or provide relevant advertisements.

You can usually control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

5. Advertising

LifeKit may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense or other advertising networks.

These services may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, personalize, measure, or limit advertisements.

Third-party advertising providers may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Third-Party Services

LifeKit may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, advertising, security, or other website functions.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. File Processing

Some LifeKit tools allow users to upload or process files, such as images or PDF documents.

Where a tool processes files directly in the browser, the files are processed locally on the user's device and are not intentionally stored by LifeKit.

Users should avoid uploading sensitive or confidential information to online services unless they understand how that service processes data.

8. Children's Privacy

LifeKit is intended to provide general-purpose online tools and does not knowingly request personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect the website and information handled through it. However, no internet service can guarantee complete security.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to LifeKit, its services, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new update date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or LifeKit's privacy practices, you may contact the LifeKit website owner.
