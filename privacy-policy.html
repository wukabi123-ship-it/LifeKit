Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to LifeKit. LifeKit provides simple online tools designed to help users complete everyday tasks quickly and easily.

We respect your privacy and aim to explain clearly how information may be handled when you use our website.

2. Information We Collect

LifeKit does not require users to create an account to use most of its tools.

Some LifeKit tools process files directly in your web browser. Where this occurs, the file processing is performed locally on your device and the file is not intentionally stored by LifeKit as part of that browser-based processing.

Like most websites, LifeKit may receive limited technical information through normal web requests and technologies. This may include information such as browser type, device type, operating system, IP address, referring page, pages requested, and basic technical information needed to operate and secure the website.

3. How We Use Information

Information that may be processed through the website can be used for purposes such as:

  • Operating and maintaining LifeKit.
  • Improving website performance and usability.
  • Understanding website usage and technical performance.
  • Detecting errors, abuse, fraud, and security problems.
  • Providing or supporting advertising and analytics services where applicable.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

LifeKit and third-party services used on the website may use cookies, local storage, pixels, or similar technologies.

These technologies may be used for functions such as remembering settings, measuring website usage, maintaining security, understanding performance, and supporting advertising.

You can usually control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking or disabling certain cookies may affect the operation of some websites or features.

5. Advertising

LifeKit may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's previous visits to LifeKit or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies allows Google and its advertising partners to provide advertisements based on a user's visits to websites, including LifeKit, and other websites on the internet.

Users may manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings:

Google Ads Settings

For additional information about how Google handles information when it provides advertising and other services on partner websites, please review Google's privacy information.

Other third-party advertising providers or advertising networks may also be used in the future. Where applicable, their use of cookies and similar technologies may be described in this policy or through the relevant consent and privacy mechanisms.

6. Third-Party Services

LifeKit may use third-party services for purposes such as hosting, content delivery, analytics, advertising, security, file-processing libraries, or other website functions.

These third-party services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Some LifeKit tools use browser-based libraries or resources provided by third-party content delivery services. Your browser may therefore make requests to those services when a relevant tool is loaded.

7. File Processing

Some LifeKit tools allow users to process files such as images and PDF documents.

Where a tool is designed to process files directly in the browser, the file-processing operation takes place locally on the user's device. LifeKit does not intentionally store those files on its servers as part of that browser-based process.

Because websites and browsers operate over the internet, users should avoid submitting sensitive or confidential information unless they are comfortable with the way the relevant website and third-party services operate.

8. Children's Privacy

LifeKit is a general-purpose website providing online utility tools. The website does not knowingly request personal information from children for the purpose of creating user accounts.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact the LifeKit website owner so that the matter can be reviewed.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect the website and information processed through it. However, no website, transmission over the internet, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your Choices and Privacy Controls

Depending on your location and the services available on the website, you may have choices regarding cookies, personalized advertising, and certain data-processing activities.

You can control cookies through your browser settings and can manage personalized Google advertising through Google's Ads Settings.

Where consent is required by applicable law or advertising requirements, LifeKit or its advertising partners may provide an appropriate consent or privacy message before certain advertising technologies are used.

11. International Users

LifeKit may be accessed by users in different countries. Depending on where you are located, different privacy laws and user rights may apply.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page together with a revised update date.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or LifeKit's privacy practices, you may contact the LifeKit website owner through the Contact page.
