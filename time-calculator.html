What Can This Time Calculator Calculate?

LifeKit can calculate the duration between two times or between two complete date-and-time values.

  • Time differences within the same day.
  • Durations that cross midnight.
  • Differences between two calendar dates.
  • Differences between two dates and specific times.

How to Calculate Between Two Dates and Times

Choose “Date + Time”, enter the Day, Month and Year separately, or select the date from the calendar.

  • Enter the starting day.
  • Enter the starting month.
  • Enter the starting year.
  • Repeat the same process for the ending date.
  • Enter the start and end times.
  • Click “Calculate Duration”.
Date range LifeKit supports years from 1 to 9999 in Date + Time mode.

Multiple Ways to Read the Result

The same duration can be useful in different forms depending on what you are trying to calculate.

  • Years, months and days: Useful for understanding a calendar-based period.
  • Total days: Useful when comparing longer periods.
  • Total weeks: Useful for weekly schedules and planning.
  • Total hours: Useful when working with elapsed hours.
  • Total minutes: Useful for minute-based calculations.
  • Total seconds: Useful when a calculation needs a smaller unit.

Why Calendar Results and Total Results Are Different

A calendar-based result such as “1 year, 2 months and 5 days” describes the period using calendar units. Total days and total hours describe the elapsed duration as one numerical value.

Calendar months are not all the same length, so converting every month into one fixed number of days would not represent a calendar period accurately.

Common Uses

  • Measuring how long a project has lasted.
  • Finding the duration between appointments or events.
  • Calculating travel or trip durations.
  • Checking study periods and schedules.
  • Finding the time between two important dates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can this calculator calculate between two dates?

Yes. Select “Date + Time” and enter or select the starting and ending dates and times.

Can I choose a date from a calendar?

Yes. Click the calendar button below the Day, Month and Year fields and select the date you need.

Can I calculate between different years?

Yes. Date + Time mode supports years from 1 through 9999.

What happens if I enter an invalid date?

LifeKit checks the actual calendar date. For example, 31 February is rejected, while 29 February is accepted in a leap year.

What forms of result are shown?

LifeKit shows a calendar-style breakdown together with total days, weeks, hours, minutes, decimal hours, and seconds.

Do I still need the Next Day option?

The Next Day option is used in Time Only mode. In Date + Time mode, select the correct ending date instead.
